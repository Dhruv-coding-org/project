3. **That's it!** A small black window will open in the background, and your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox) will automatically pop open to **[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)**!

1. Press **`Ctrl + Shift + B`** on your keyboard.
2. Select **`🚀 1. Start CyberZero Web Academy Server`**.
3. Hold **`Ctrl`** and click `http://localhost:8000` in the terminal to open your browser!

**Option B: Manual IDE Terminal**:
1. Open the IDE Terminal (Press `` Ctrl + ` `` or top menu **Terminal -> New Terminal**).
